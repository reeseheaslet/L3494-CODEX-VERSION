About Our Union

Local 3494 represents the professional firefighters of the City of Davis Fire Department. Founded to protect the rights and welfare of our members, we are committed to ensuring safe working conditions, fair compensation, and excellence in public safety.

Our members serve the Davis community around the clock, responding to emergencies with professionalism and dedication. The union advocates on behalf of every member — from contract negotiations to workplace safety standards.
