About Us

Professional firefighters serving the City of Davis

Our Mission

Dedicated to Service

Local 3494 represents the professional firefighters of the City of Davis Fire Department. We strive to foster and cultivate a great relationship between the citizens of Davis and the firefighters who keep it safe. Our members serve the Davis community around the clock, responding to emergencies with professionalism and dedication.

Community Involvement

Giving Back to Davis

Our dedication to the city doesn't stop when we are off duty. We continue to work to help through various programs such as Turkey Basket Drive, Local BBQs, Toys for Tots, and our annual Crab Feast in partnership with Thriving Pink.

Our Commitment

Building a Better Davis

Together, with the city council and city leadership, we are committed and working towards making Davis a safer, vibrant, and inclusive place for all.
